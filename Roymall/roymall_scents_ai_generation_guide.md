# AI Generation Guide: Roymall Scents Luxury E-commerce

To replicate this exact design style and structure in an AI design tool, use the following prompt and resource links.

## 1. The Master Design Prompt

**Copy and paste this into the AI prompt field:**

> "Build a high-fidelity, luxury e-commerce platform for 'Roymall Scents,' a premium perfume brand. 
>
> **Visual Identity:** 
> - Use a 'Roymall Luxury' theme: Deep Regal Navy (#001B44) as the primary brand color, accented with Metallic Gold (#D4AF37) for calls-to-action, borders, and high-priority labels. 
> - Typography: Use 'Playfair Display' for all headlines to convey elegance, and a clean sans-serif (like Montserrat or Inter) for body text and administrative data.
> - Aesthetic: Minimalist luxury with high-contrast layouts, generous whitespace, and sharp, thin borders.
>
> **Required Screens:**
> 1. **Storefront (Desktop & Mobile):** Hero section with 'Established Luxury' messaging, 'New Arrivals' grid, and 'Curated Collections' (Pour Homme, Pour Femme, L'Essence Unisexe).
> 2. **Admin Portal (Desktop):** A professional management suite including a Dashboard (KPI cards for sales/orders), Inventory Management (product table with SKU, stock status, and pricing in Naira), Order Tracking, Customer Directory (VIP segmentation), and Analytics.
>
> **Specific Constraints:**
> - Pricing must be displayed in Nigerian Naira (₦).
> - Navigation must include a persistent sidebar for Admin and a clean, centered Top Bar for the storefront.
> - Integration: Use the structure and professional commerce flow inspired by fragranceforte.com.ng but elevated with the Roymall Navy/Gold palette."

## 2. Reference Links for the AI

Provide these URLs to the AI as "Reference Material" or "Style Inspiration":

1. **Structure & Flow Reference:** `https://fragranceforte.com.ng/` (Tell the AI to extract the e-commerce architecture, product categorization, and checkout flow from here).
2. **Visual Asset Reference:** Provide the logo and brand imagery generated in this session to ensure the AI uses the correct logo and product photography.

## 3. Implementation Instructions

If you are using an agentic AI (like Stitch), follow this order of operations:
1. **Design System First:** Ask it to "Generate a design system based on Roymall Scents logo and fragranceforte.com.ng."
2. **Component Prediction:** Ask it to "Predict shared components for a multi-screen luxury shop and admin portal."
3. **Screen Generation:** Finally, ask it to "Generate the storefront and admin screens using the established design system."
